Safety Directive OPS-BUL-2024-08

Effective Date: August 12, 2024

Department: Flight Operations Directorate

Subject: Severe Turbulence Penetration Speeds & Passenger Safety Protocol

1. Operational Directives

All flight crews operating within oceanic and convective weather corridors must adhere to the following:

  • Immediately illuminate FASTEN SEATBELT signs upon forecast or visual indication of convective activity.
  • Instruct cabin crew to stow service carts and take jumpseats immediately.
  • Target turbulence penetration airspeed: 280 KIAS or 0.76M (whichever is lower).

2. Reporting Protocol

Crews experiencing moderate or severe turbulence must transmit an immediate PIREP to ATC and submit a flight safety report within 24 hours of arrival.
