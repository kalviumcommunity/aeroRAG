FLIGHT OPERATIONS SAFETY BULLETIN

BULLETIN NO: OPS-BUL-2024-08

SUBJECT: Severe Turbulence Penetration Procedures and Airspeed Targets

EFFECTIVE DATE: 2024-03-20

STATUS: CURRENT

APPLICABILITY: All Commercial Jet Fleet (B737, B777, A320)

1. TURBULENCE PENETRATION AIRSPEED LIMITS

When penetrating severe clear-air turbulence (CAT) or convective mountain wave activity, pilots must adhere strictly to recommended target speeds. Do NOT chase indicated airspeed fluctuations on the Primary Flight Display.

  • Target Airspeed: Maintain 280 KIAS or 0.76 Mach, whichever is lower.
  • Pitch Attitude: Fly attitude control with reference to the Artificial Horizon / Attitude Indicator. Allow airspeed and altitude to vary; excessive control inputs may overstress flight controls and airframe structures.
  • Engine Ignition: Switch engine start switches to CONT (Continuous) or FLT to protect against engine flameout from compressor stalls.
  • Autopilot / Autothrottle: Disengage Autothrottle. Disengage Autopilot Altitude Hold (ALT HLD) mode to avoid aggressive pitch corrections during updrafts or downdrafts. Heading Select (HDG SEL) and basic Attitude Hold may remain engaged.

2. CABIN SAFETY DIRECTIVES

Immediate actions upon encountering moderate or severe turbulence:

  1. Turn FASTEN SEAT BELT signs ON.
  2. Make mandatory PA announcement: "FLIGHT ATTENDANTS BE SEATED IMMEDIATELY".
  3. Suspend all cabin service, hot beverage distribution, and secure galley carts.
