# FEDERAL AVIATION ADMINISTRATION REGULATORY DIRECTIVE
## 14 CFR PART 121.639 — FUEL REQUIREMENTS FOR DOMESTIC IFR FLIGHTS
DOCUMENT ID: FAR-PART-121-639
VERSION: REG-2023.1
EFFECTIVE DATE: 2023-01-01
STATUS: CURRENT
DOCUMENT TYPE: Regulatory Directive

### 1. GENERAL DOMESTIC DISPATCH FUEL RESERVES
No person may dispatch or take off an airplane in domestic air carrier operations unless it carries enough fuel:
(a) To fly to the airport to which it is dispatched (Trip Fuel);
(b) Thereafter, to fly to and land at the most distant alternate airport specified in the dispatch release (Alternate Fuel); and
(c) Thereafter, to fly for 45 minutes at normal cruising fuel consumption rate (Reserve Fuel).

### 2. DISPATCH CONTINGENCY FUEL
When destination weather indicates adverse conditions, convective SIGMETs, or expected air traffic holding delays:
- Dispatchers must add minimum 15 minutes of contingency fuel.
- In terminal areas with Ground Delay Programs (GDP), required expected delay fuel must be loaded in addition to the 45-minute statutory reserve.
- Flight crew may not accept a dispatch release if the planned fuel at touchdown is less than the 45-minute FAR reserve requirement.
