STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE: DISPATCH FUEL RESERVES
Document Reference: SOP-FUEL-009
Effective Date: 2023-11-01
Authority: FAA FAR Part 121.639 Compliance

1. Domestic IFR Fuel Requirements:
No pilot may dispatch or takeoff an aircraft under IFR unless it carries sufficient fuel to:
a. Fly to the destination airport specified in the dispatch release.
b. Fly to and land at the most distant alternate airport specified in the dispatch release.
c. Thereafter, fly for a minimum of 45 minutes at normal cruising fuel consumption rate.

2. Contingency Reserves:
Dispatchers must plan an additional 5% contingency fuel on scheduled flight times exceeding 3 hours or whenever destination terminal weather forecasts crosswinds exceeding 20 knots.

3. Captain Discretionary Fuel:
The Pilot-in-Command reserves authority to request up to 1,500 lbs of additional discretionary fuel without prior dispatcher re-release.
