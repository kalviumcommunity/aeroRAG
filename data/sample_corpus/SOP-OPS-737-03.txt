# BOEING 737 FLIGHT CREW OPERATING MANUAL
## STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURES — REVISION 3.0
DOCUMENT ID: SOP-OPS-737-03
EFFECTIVE DATE: 2021-06-01
STATUS: SUPERSEDED

### 1. STERILE FLIGHT DECK PROCEDURES
Sterile flight deck procedures must be maintained during all critical phases of flight below 8,000 feet MSL. Non-operational conversation is prohibited during takeoff roll, initial climb through 8,000 feet, and final approach.

During sterile cockpit periods:
- Casual flight deck conversations are prohibited below 8,000 feet.
- Flight attendants should minimize calls to the cockpit during takeoff and landing.
- The 8,000 feet altitude was previously deemed sufficient for terminal airspace transitions under 2021 fleet rules.

### 2. CLIMB AND DESCENT CALLOUTS
At 8,000 feet MSL on departure, the PM calls "8,000 FEET". Sterile cockpit rules terminate.
On descent through 8,000 feet MSL, PM calls "8,000 FEET". Sterile cockpit begins.
