### EMERGENCY DESCENT CHECKLIST

Condition: Rapid cabin depressurization or catastrophic loss of cabin pressure at altitude.

#### Immediate Action Items (Memory Items):
1. **OXYGEN MASKS AND REGULATORS**: DON 100% EMERGENCY FLOW (Both Pilots).
2. **CREW COMMUNICATIONS**: ESTABLISH via flight interphone.
3. **PASSENGER OXYGEN SWITCH**: ON (Deploy passenger masks if cabin altitude exceeds 14,000 feet).
4. **THRUST LEVERS**: CLOSE TO IDLE.
5. **AUTOTHROTTLE**: DISENGAGE.
6. **SPEEDBRAKE LEVER**: EXTEND FULLY.
7. **DESCENT TARGET ALTITUDE**: Set MCP altitude window to 10,000 FEET or Minimum Off-Route Altitude (MORA), whichever is higher.
8. **AIRSPEED**: Maintain Vmo/Mmo (Maximum Operating Speed) unless structural damage is suspected. If structural turbulence or airframe damage is suspected, maintain 250 KIAS.
9. **TRANSPONDER**: SQUAWK 7700.
10. **ATC NOTIFICATION**: Declare "MAYDAY, MAYDAY, MAYDAY - EMERGENCY DESCENT IN PROGRESS".

Level Off Procedure:
- At 2,000 feet above target altitude: Smoothly retract speedbrakes.
- At 1,000 feet above target altitude: Advance thrust levers to maintain 250 knots.
- Once leveled at 10,000 feet or MEA: Check cabin altitude; remove flight crew oxygen masks when cabin altitude is verified below 10,000 feet.
