# Quick Reference Handbook (QRH) - Emergency Descent

**Document ID:** QRH-EMER-01  
**Aircraft Type:** Boeing 737-800 / MAX 8  
**Effective Date:** 2024-01-15  
**Status:** APPROVED  

---

## Announce & Initiate
1. **Oxygen Masks:** ON / 100% (Flight Crew)
2. **Crew Communications:** ESTABLISH
3. **Passenger Signs:** ON
4. **Altitude Selector:** SET to Minimum Enroute Altitude (MEA) or 10,000 feet, whichever is higher
5. **Thrust Levers:** CLOSE
6. **Speedbrake Flight Detent:** EXTEND smoothly

## Airspeed Target
- If structural integrity is in doubt: **Maintain current speed, do not exceed limit.**
- If structural integrity is assured: **Descend at Vmo / Mmo.**

## Level Off
- Level off at 10,000 feet MSL or MEA.
- Disengage speedbrake slowly before level off.
- Set thrust to maintain level flight.
