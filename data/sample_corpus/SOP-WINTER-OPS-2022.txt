### 1. HOLDOVER TIMES (HOT) FOR TYPE I AND TYPE IV FLUIDS
Caution: This procedure expired on 2023-04-30. Revised seasonal holdover tables are published annually by Flight Operations Engineering.

Holdover table guidelines:
- Type I fluid (50/50 mix heated at 60C): Active frost at -3C to 0C provides 45 minutes holdover protection.
- Freezing fog at -4C: Type I fluid provides 11 to 17 minutes holdover protection.
- Type IV anti-icing fluid: Active snow provides 35 to 75 minutes holdover time depending on precipitation intensity.

### 2. PRE-TAKEOFF CONTAMINATION CHECK
If holdover time expires prior to takeoff:
- Flight crew must request a secondary de-icing spray or perform a pre-takeoff contamination inspection from the cabin windows within 5 minutes of takeoff roll.
- If wing contamination (snow, frost, or slush) is visually detected on upper wing surfaces or control tabs, takeoff is strictly prohibited until a complete de-icing cycle is repeated.
